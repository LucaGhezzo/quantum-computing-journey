# Quantum Computing Journey

Jupyter notebooks and practical exercises documenting my self-study path through quantum computing, built with [Qiskit](https://www.ibm.com/quantum/qiskit).

I'm a physics undergraduate (University of Padova) working through a structured curriculum — qubits, quantum gates, entanglement, and measurement, moving toward quantum algorithms (Deutsch-Jozsa, Grover, Shor), variational algorithms (VQE, QAOA), quantum machine learning, error correction, and real IBM hardware — as part of my preparation for graduate studies in Quantum Engineering and the IBM Quantum Computation certification.

## Structure

- `circuits/` — Jupyter notebooks with implemented circuits, one per topic covered.
- `output/` — weekly study log tracking topics covered session by session.

## Status

Early stage — updated incrementally as I progress through the study plan.
